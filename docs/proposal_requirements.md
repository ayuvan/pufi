# PUFI --- Hackathon Proposal Alignment

## Requirements confirmed from the supplied screenshots

### What to build

Build or update an application integrating: - PayPal developer platform
using the free Sandbox environment. - An AI tool, model, or platform of
choice.

Sponsor tools may also be used but are optional. The screenshot lists AG
Grid, APIMatic, Astropods, Bryntum, Channel3, Elastic, KERNEL, Postman,
Render, and Zapier.

### Project should

-   Meaningfully integrate at least one PayPal
    technology/API/SDK/product/developer capability.
-   Meaningfully incorporate AI into the experience or functionality.
-   Demonstrate a working prototype or proof of concept.
-   Be sufficiently documented for judges.
-   Comply with official rules.

Projects may be individual or team projects and may be new or existing,
provided meaningful progress is made during the hackathon.

## Proposed judge demo

1.  Introduce the enterprise supplier-payment problem.
2.  Import a synthetic batch.
3.  Show duplicate and invalid-record detection.
4.  Show FX assumptions and provider cost comparison.
5.  Show AI explanation and anomaly flags.
6.  Explain that deterministic controls retain authority.
7.  Approve and submit a supported PayPal Sandbox test payout, if
    account/API access permits.
8.  Show provider response and internal reconciliation.
9.  Show simulated provider and settlement records clearly labelled.
10. State limitations and future work.

## Claims discipline

Avoid saying: - "PUFI makes PayPal hold more money." - "PUFI creates a
new international payment rail." - "PUFI guarantees cheaper or instant
transfers." - "PUFI settles USD to INR through PayPal" unless current
provider documentation and account tests establish it. - "AI
autonomously routes and pays suppliers."

Prefer: - "PUFI coordinates enterprise payment obligations across
eligible provider integrations." - "The prototype compares configured
cost and FX scenarios." - "AI explains trade-offs and flags anomalies;
deterministic rules enforce eligibility and approval." - "PayPal Sandbox
demonstrates the provider integration; other routes and external
settlement are simulated." - "The prototype evaluates whether
coordination and reconciliation can reduce operational overhead."
